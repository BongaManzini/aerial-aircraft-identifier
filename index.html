Why Top-Down Aerial Detection is Hard:

In standard street-level computer vision, aircraft are seen from the side with clear windows and tail fins. In aerial and satellite imagery, orientation is completely arbitrary ($0^\circ$ to $360^\circ$) and tarmac line markings can mimic wings. AeroScan AI uses rotational invariance and contour aspect ratios to reliably distinguish aircraft even against high runway glare.

UniSA Hackathon Thought Process

Engineering Philosophy & Model Design

The Problem Statement

Create a fast, accessible platform where anyone can upload aerial satellite photographs of airfields and immediately get verified aircraft classifications, bounding boxes, and aerodynamic profiles without expensive GPU server infrastructure.

Neural Network Architecture

Engineered with a lightweight MobileNetV2 / ResNet backbone quantized to ONNX/TensorFlow.js. Trained on aerial datasets (DOTA & MAR20) with aggressive random rotation, affine warping, and Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE).

Real-World Application

Useful for disaster response monitoring (checking how many cargo transports or helicopters are deployed on emergency runways), border surveillance, and aviation enthusiast cataloging across Southern African aerodromes.
